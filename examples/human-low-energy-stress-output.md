# Human Low-Energy Stress Output — Backtrace Sample

## Observable Output

Persistent fatigue, cold intolerance, unstable appetite rhythm, and lean-mass loss concern over ~8 weeks—described as “low energy” stress output pattern, not a clinical diagnosis in this sample.

## Minimal Context

- Branch: Human FBM
- Window: ~8 weeks after self-initiated aggressive calorie reduction while reducing carbohydrate visibly on labels
- Activity: maintained exercise volume despite intake drop
- No medication changes in sample window
- Structural note only: **exogenous carbohydrate load** reduced on paper while **total energy** may be insufficient; no calorie numbers disclosed

## Backtrace Chain

1. **Observable output:** fatigue, cold intolerance, appetite instability, lean-mass concern  
2. **Sufficiency layer:** **insufficient total energy** relative to demand → stress compensation  
3. **Mass layer:** lean-mass risk and unstable metabolic output  
4. **Scheduling layer:** attempted low-carb framing without stable **fatty-acid direct energy** and **insulin regulation state** coherence  
5. **Upstream input:** **food structure** and energy sufficiency boundary—not “willpower” or generic calorie moralism

## Dominant Structural Variable

**Energy sufficiency boundary** violation — Human FBM requires sufficient total energy concurrently with low exogenous carbohydrate input and low insulin occupancy. Calorie-restriction identity without sufficiency produces stress output, not stable Human FBM.

## Why Other Explanations Fail

| Mis-framing | Why it fails in FBM |
|-------------|---------------------|
| “Low carb equals Human FBM” | Carbohydrate reduction alone does not establish structure if total energy collapses |
| Weight-loss method | Human FBM is **long-term metabolic operating structure**, not deprivation for weight loss |
| Calorie-control method | FBM evaluates **substrate dominance and insulin regulation**, not calorie slogans in isolation |
| Ketogenic diet identity | Ketone framing ≠ structural sufficiency and fatty-acid direct energy stability |
| Thyroid / adrenal as assumed root | Endocrine disease requires clinical boundary; this sample stops at structural sufficiency failure |

## FBM Interpretation

The output pattern indexes **stress compensation** from **insufficient total energy** while the subject may believe they are “doing low carb correctly.” Human FBM valid state requires: low exogenous carbohydrate input, reduced long-term insulin occupancy, **fatty-acid direct energy**, **sufficient total energy**, and lean-mass stability **together**.

This sample treats low energy as **backtrace entry**, not as proof that “FBM failed”—it may indicate the operating structure was never established.

## Update Criterion

Revisit interpretation if, over a comparable window:

- Total energy sufficiency is restored at structure level and stress outputs trend toward prior stable baseline, or  
- Clinical evaluation reclassifies (e.g., endocrine disorder), or  
- Lean-mass and appetite stabilize without changing the stated structural variables → reconsider dominant variable assignment

- Enter **medical workflow** if: unexplained rapid weight loss, syncope, chest pain, or diagnosed eating disorder  
- This sample is **not** a diet prescription, **not** a weight-loss plan, and **not** medical advice
