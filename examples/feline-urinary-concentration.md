# Feline Urinary Concentration — Backtrace Sample

## Observable Output

Urine appears consistently more concentrated than baseline for this cat over a multi-week window (higher specific-gravity indexing pattern reported by caregiver observation—not a single lab diagnosis).

## Minimal Context

- Species: adult cat (Feline FBM)
- Window: ~4 weeks stable diet period before observation shift
- No medication changes reported in sample window
- Structural note only: chronic food structure previously aligned with elevated **protein energy pressure** indexing; no formula or ratio disclosed here

## Backtrace Chain

1. **Observable output:** urinary concentration pattern  
2. **Renal layer:** elevated **solute load per unit energy**; **nitrogen processing pressure**  
3. **Energy layer:** protein carrying excess energy-supply duty relative to stable fat-substrate dominance  
4. **Regulation layer:** **insulin regulation state** and substrate scheduling not aligned with obligate-carnivore structure expectation  
5. **Upstream input:** **food structure** — not drinking volume alone

## Dominant Structural Variable

**Solute load per unit energy** — nitrogenous burden per unit usable energy rises when protein is structurally pushed into chronic energy-supply roles while fat fails to stabilize as **primary energy substrate**.

## FBM Interpretation

Urinary concentration here is a **backtrace entry** into renal solute handling under current **food structure**. The analytic question is whether structural misalignment chronically elevates **protein energy pressure** and **solute load per unit energy**, not whether the cat can be “hydrated into” a different structural load.

Water intake may modulate dilution after structure is understood; it is not substituted as the upstream cause in this sample.

## Update Criterion

Revisit interpretation if, over a comparable window:

- **Food structure** changes materially (documented at structure level, not by brand slogan), or  
- Observable concentration pattern normalizes while structure and solute-load proxies move together, or  
- Clinical diagnostics reclassify the case (see boundary below)

## Boundary / Reclassification Conditions

- Enter **clinical workflow** if: pain on urination, hematuria, obstruction signs, anuria, or acute collapse  
- Suspend structure-only backtrace if: diagnosed renal disease, diabetes, or infection under active veterinary treatment  
- This sample is **not** treatment advice and **not** a product-effect claim
