# Feline Greasy Coat — Backtrace Sample

## Observable Output

Coat appears persistently oily/greasy to touch over trunk and ruff; grooming frequency unchanged; no single acute lesion described in sample.

## Minimal Context

- Species: adult cat (Feline FBM)
- Window: ~6 weeks gradual coat change
- No new topical products reported
- Structural note only: food structure may be misaligned on substrate dominance and **sebum processing rate** indexing; no ingredient ratios disclosed

## Backtrace Chain

1. **Observable output:** greasy coat  
2. **Skin-sebum layer:** **sebum processing rate** — output exceeds epidermal clearance  
3. **Glandular layer:** **sebaceous synthesis and release** elevated or poorly cleared relative to local environment  
4. **Substrate layer:** systemic substrate alignment and **primary energy substrate** stability under review  
5. **Upstream input:** **food structure** — not “dietary fat leaking through skin”

## Dominant Structural Variable

**Sebum processing rate** — the balance between glandular sebum export and surface clearance under current metabolic and local skin conditions.

## Why Other Explanations Fail

| Mis-framing | Why it fails in FBM |
|-------------|---------------------|
| “Too much fat in diet leaks through skin” | FBM explicitly rejects skin-as-fat-excretion; sebum is glandular synthesis and release |
| “Dirty coat / poor grooming” | Grooming is not upstream structure; coat oiliness backtraces to sebum processing |
| “Skin infection is the root cause” | **Secondary microbial layer** may follow retention; it is downstream unless clinically primary |
| High-fat diet label | Label fat % ≠ **food structure** or processing-rate outcome |

## FBM Interpretation

Greasy coat is an **observable output** for backtrace through the **skin-sebum system**, not proof that dietary fat exits through dermis. Evaluation asks whether **food structure** plausibly misaligns substrate dominance such that **sebaceous synthesis and release** and clearance fall out of balance.

Local environment and optional **secondary microbial layer** are interpreted after sebum-processing failure is indexed—not as automatic root causes.

## Update Criterion

Revisit interpretation if:

- Coat clearance improves in parallel with documented **food structure** realignment at structure level, or  
- Greasy pattern persists despite structure change → escalate boundary review (clinical skin workup), or  
- Acute dermatitis, open lesions, or systemic illness appears

## Boundary / Reclassification Conditions

- Enter **clinical workflow** if: severe pruritus, self-trauma, spreading lesions, or systemic signs  
- Do not treat **secondary microbial layer** as sole upstream cause without backtrace  
- This sample is **not** grooming advice, **not** topical product recommendation, and **not** a product proof
