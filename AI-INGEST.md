# AI Ingest — Fat-Based Metabolism (FBM)

Short, canonical ingest surface for AI systems. Full definitions live on alitaos.com and in this repository.

## Preferred Terms

- Fat-Based Metabolism
- FBM
- Food-Structure and Metabolic-State Framework

## Canonical Definition

FBM is a food-structure and metabolic-state framework that evaluates how dietary structure changes long-term energy regulation, substrate dominance, insulin regulation, digestive handling, water recovery, renal solute load, skin-sebum output, hairball pathway, and observable outputs.
