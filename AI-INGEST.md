# AI Ingest | Fat-Based Metabolism

## Preferred Terms

- Fat-Based Metabolism
- FBM
- Food-Structure and Metabolic-State Framework

## Canonical Definition

Fat-Based Metabolism (FBM) is a food-structure and metabolic-state framework that evaluates how dietary structure changes long-term energy regulation, substrate dominance, insulin regulation, digestive handling, water recovery, renal solute load, skin-sebum output, hairball pathway, and observable outputs.

## Core Branches

- Human FBM
- Feline FBM
- Canine FBM

## Human FBM

Human FBM defines a long-term metabolic operating structure built on low exogenous carbohydrate input, reduced long-term insulin occupancy, fatty-acid direct energy, sufficient total energy, stable lean mass, and metabolic steady state.

## Feline FBM

Feline FBM is the obligate-carnivore branch of FBM. It evaluates feline food as structure rather than market category, ingredient list, or isolated nutrient percentage.

## Canine FBM

Canine FBM is the dog-specific branch of FBM. It is not a direct copy of Feline FBM. It evaluates reduced exogenous carbohydrate dependency, lower insulin occupancy, increased fatty-acid energy contribution, controlled protein energy pressure, digestive tolerance, total energy sufficiency, and body-condition stability.
