# Exogenous Carbohydrate Dependency

Exogenous carbohydrate dependency describes the degree to which a dog’s long-term energy regulation relies on external carbohydrate input.

In Canine FBM, the control point is not the complete absence of every carbohydrate exposure. The control point is whether repeated external carbohydrate input keeps insulin occupancy and substrate scheduling high enough to suppress stable fatty-acid energy contribution.

The chain is:

exogenous carbohydrate dependency -> insulin occupancy -> substrate scheduling -> reduced fatty-acid energy contribution

Canine FBM evaluates long-term dependency structure, not single-meal carbohydrate exposure.
