# Skin-Sebum System

Feline coat and chin presentations in FBM are modeled through sebaceous processing and local environment—not through passive fat exuding through skin.

## sebaceous synthesis and release

**Sebaceous synthesis and release** is glandular production and export of sebum. Rate and composition respond to systemic substrate alignment and local skin conditions.

## sebum processing rate

**Sebum processing rate** is the balance between glandular output and epidermal clearance. When output exceeds clearance, **greasy coat** and retention-prone areas may appear as observable outputs.

## greasy coat

Greasy coat is an observable output for backtrace to sebum processing rate and substrate alignment—not proof of dietary fat "leaking" through skin.

## acne-like chin presentation

**Acne-like chin presentation** indexes local sebum retention, follicular environment, and often a **secondary microbial layer** after environment shifts—not primary infection as the structural starting point.

## local skin environment

Local pH, hydration, and clearance rhythm modulate whether sebum is processed or retained. This layer sits above food structure but below clinical diagnosis.

## secondary microbial layer

**Secondary microbial layer** describes microbial colonization that follows altered sebum or retention conditions. It is downstream; treating only the microbial layer without backtracing structure misaligns causality.

## Explicit Prohibition

**Fat does not leak through skin** as the explanatory model in FBM. Sebum is synthesized and released by sebaceous glands; evaluation uses **sebum processing rate**, not dermal fat leakage.
