# Digestion and Fat Transport

Feline fat handling is a sequential pipeline. **Fat processing rate** is the effective throughput of the whole chain—not fat percentage on a label alone.

## Gastric Release

Stomach delivery rhythm affects how fat and protein enter duodenal processing. Release patterns interact with **gastric emptying speed** in the hairball pathway and with downstream emulsification load.

## Bile Salt Dispersion

**Bile salt dispersion** emulsifies dietary fat into droplets for enzymatic access. Incomplete dispersion reduces absorbable surface area and alters bile-acid flux to the colon.

## Pancreatic Enzyme Processing

Lipolytic and proteolytic processing in the small intestine cleaves macronutrients for absorption. Enzyme demand rises with structural load; misaligned structure can stress processing capacity relative to **fat processing rate**.

## Small-Intestinal Absorption

Absorption of fatty acids and monoglycerides occurs at the mucosal surface after emulsification and cleavage. Upstream dispersion and enzyme steps constrain absorption—not the reverse.

## Lymphatic Transport

Long-chain fat transport via lymphatic route delivers lipid to systemic circulation. Transport efficiency is part of **fat processing rate** indexing.

## fat processing rate (Summary)

**Fat processing rate** integrates:

- Gastric release timing
- Bile salt dispersion quality
- Pancreatic enzyme throughput
- Mucosal absorption
- Lymphatic transport

Observable digestive outputs (e.g., stool form) backtrace to this layer when structure loads fat handling beyond stable rate.
