# Energy Regulation

Feline energy regulation in FBM is evaluated through structural layers—not through ketone targets or single-nutrient percentages.

## primary energy substrate

The macronutrient class that chronically supplies most usable energy. Species-aligned feline structure expects fat as **primary energy substrate**. Misalignment shifts burden to protein or elevates carbohydrate-mediated regulation.

## exogenous carbohydrate load

**Exogenous carbohydrate load** is dietary carbohydrate entering insulin-regulated pathways. Obligate carnivore structure expects low chronic load. Elevated load increases insulin occupancy and competes with stable fat oxidation.

## insulin regulation state

**Insulin regulation state** is the long-term partitioning posture: which substrates are promoted or suppressed at the systemic level. It is an output of food structure, not a standalone treatment target in FBM.

## fatty acid oxidation pathway

The **fatty acid oxidation pathway** converts fatty acids to usable energy when substrate dominance and insulin state permit. Chronic insulin elevation or protein energy pressure can suppress stable operation even when dietary fat appears adequate on a label.

## protein energy pressure

**Protein energy pressure** rises when protein supplies energy beyond structural amino needs—often when fat is not structurally dominant or carbohydrate structure misaligns. This pressure connects to nitrogen handling and renal solute load.

## FBM vs Ketogenic Framing

Ketone level is a liver processing output. FBM asks whether fat **structurally** dominates energy supply with aligned insulin and oxidation pathways—not whether ketones reach a diet-culture threshold.
