# Hairball Pathway

Hairball events are pathway outputs indexed through gastric dynamics—not independent diseases named only by vomit appearance.

## gastric emptying speed

**Gastric emptying speed** controls how quickly gastric contents—including ingested hair—enter duodenal processing. Slower emptying increases **gastric hair residence time**.

## gastric hair residence time

Longer residence increases opportunity for hair to interact with gastric contents and motility patterns.

## hair entanglement probability

**Hair entanglement probability** rises with residence time, content consistency, and motility rhythm. Entanglement is a pathway probability, not a moral or grooming failure label.

## regurgitation probability

**Regurgitation probability** is the likelihood of hairball vomit as an observable output given emptying speed, residence time, and entanglement. FBM backtraces these layers to structural and motility context.

## physiological vs pathological gastric motility boundary

**Physiological gastric motility slowing** (e.g., post-meal rhythm variation within normal range) must be distinguished from **pathological gastric motility abnormality** (disease-level dysmotility). FBM provides structural reasoning only; crossing into diagnosed disease is a **clinical boundary** matter.
