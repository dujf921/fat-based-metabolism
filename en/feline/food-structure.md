# Food Structure

## Ingredient List ≠ Food Structure

A label ingredient sequence or guaranteed analysis table is not equivalent to **food structure**. Structure is how the composed food operates inside the feline digestive and regulatory systems over time:

- Which substrate chronically dominates energy supply
- What insulin regulation state the structure tends to produce
- What digestive and absorptive load is imposed
- How downstream water, renal, skin, and gastric pathways are stressed

Market category (dry, wet, raw, prescription marketing tier) also does not define structure.

## Structure Determines Downstream Operation

**Food structure determines downstream metabolic operation.** Once structure is set, the following layers are constrained—not independently chosen by the observer:

1. Primary energy substrate and fatty acid oxidation pathway
2. Insulin regulation state
3. Protein energy pressure and solute load per unit energy
4. Digestive handling and fat processing rate
5. Water recovery and colonic regulation
6. Renal solute handling
7. Skin-sebum processing
8. Hairball pathway dynamics
9. Observable outputs

Evaluation therefore begins at structure, not at the symptom name.

## Structural Misalignment (Conceptual)

Without disclosing formulas or ratios, common structural misalignments in feline evaluation include:

- Exogenous carbohydrate load elevated relative to obligate carnivore expectation
- Fat present on label but not structurally available as stable primary substrate
- Protein forced into chronic energy-supply roles
- Digestive processing demand exceeding stable fat processing rate
