# Feline FBM Boundaries

## emergency boundary

Acute distress, obstruction suspicion, repeated unproductive vomiting, collapse, or other emergency signs require immediate veterinary care. FBM does not substitute for emergency intervention.

## clinical diagnosis boundary

FBM provides food-structure and mechanism reasoning. It does not diagnose disease, prescribe treatment, or replace clinical examination and laboratory interpretation.

## diagnosed disease boundary

For cats with diagnosed conditions (renal disease, diabetes, inflammatory bowel disease, etc.), clinical management stays with veterinary medicine. FBM structural language does not override treatment plans.

## formula disclosure boundary

This repository and public FBM materials do not disclose formula ratios, manufacturing parameters, proprietary blends, or production control variables.

## species boundary

Feline FBM applies to **cats** as obligate carnivores. Canine or human FBM branches are separate species applications of the parent framework—not interchangeable with feline structure.

## effect guarantee boundary

FBM does not guarantee outcomes, timelines, or symptom resolution. It is a structural definition system, not an effect promise or product claim engine.
