# Renal Solute Load

Renal handling in Feline FBM is indexed through structural load per unit energy—not through encouraging water intake as if drinking were the upstream cause of concentration patterns.

## solute load per unit energy

**Solute load per unit energy** is the processing burden of nitrogenous and mineral solutes relative to energy delivered. Elevated **protein energy pressure** and misaligned food structure increase this load in cats.

## nitrogen processing pressure

When protein is chronically diverted into energy-supply roles, nitrogen handling demand rises. This is a mechanism layer connecting energy regulation to renal stress indexing—not a call to restrict water.

## urinary concentration

**Urinary concentration** (as an observable output) backtraces to solute load, nitrogen processing pressure, and systemic water modulation. It is not defined solely by how much water the cat drinks.

## drinking water as modulation variable

**Drinking water** modulates hydration and dilution downstream—it is not the upstream root cause of structural renal load. FBM evaluates food structure and solute load first; water intake is a secondary modulation variable in the model.
