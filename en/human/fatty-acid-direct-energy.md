# Fatty-Acid Direct Energy

Fatty-acid direct energy describes the operating state in which fatty acids carry the dominant long-term energy role without requiring repeated external carbohydrate scheduling.

This is not the same as a high-fat-diet label. A diet can be high in fat but still fail to create a stable Human FBM state if energy is insufficient, lean mass is lost, insulin occupancy remains high, or the system is under clinical boundary conditions.

The control chain is:

low insulin occupancy → fat mobilization access → fatty-acid availability → direct energy use → long-term energy stability

Human FBM therefore evaluates the system by substrate availability and steady-state function, not by food label or diet identity.
