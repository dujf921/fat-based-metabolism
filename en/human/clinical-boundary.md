# Clinical Boundary

Human FBM is not a clinical diagnosis system, not a treatment protocol, and not a replacement for medical care.

The framework describes food structure, substrate dominance, insulin regulation state, fatty-acid direct energy, energy sufficiency, and steady-state modeling.

Clinical boundary conditions include diagnosed disease, medication effects, pregnancy, eating-disorder risk, severe underweight state, uncontrolled endocrine disease, renal disease, liver disease, acute illness, or any condition requiring medical supervision.

When clinical boundary conditions are present, Human FBM can only function as structural nutrition language. It cannot be used as diagnosis, cure, treatment, or medical override.
