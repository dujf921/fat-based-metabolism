# Body Fat and Lean Mass Model

Human FBM uses a base state model built on body fat (F), lean mass (L), energy intake (I), and energy expenditure (E).

The primary steady-state rule is:

same metabolic base + same input structure → same stable operating tendency

Body fat (F) is the stored energy pool. Lean mass (L) is the structural and metabolic machinery that must be preserved. Energy intake (I) and energy expenditure (E) define the current energy balance, but the substrate structure determines which energy pool is accessible.

Human FBM does not reduce the system to weight alone. Weight change can reflect fat change, lean-mass change, water change, or digestive content change. The controlling structure is whether fatty-acid energy can carry long-term demand while lean mass remains stable.
