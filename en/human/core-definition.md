# Core Definition

Human Fat-Based Metabolism (Human FBM) is a long-term metabolic operating structure in which fatty acids become the dominant direct energy substrate under low exogenous carbohydrate input and low insulin occupancy.

The base model is:

low exogenous carbohydrate input → low insulin occupancy → fatty-acid direct energy → sufficient total energy → stable lean mass → long-term metabolic steady state

Human FBM evaluates a system through body fat (F), lean mass (L), energy intake (I), energy expenditure (E), insulin occupancy, leptin feedback, and energy sufficiency.

It does not define health by diet identity, calorie arithmetic alone, short-term weight change, ketone production, or social diet category.
