# Energy Sufficiency Boundary

Energy sufficiency is a required boundary condition for Human FBM.

A low-carbohydrate structure without sufficient total energy can collapse into stress output, lean-mass loss, fatigue, cold intolerance, or unstable feeding behavior. That state is not a stable Human FBM model.

The boundary chain is:

insufficient total energy → stress compensation → lean-mass risk → unstable metabolic output

Human FBM therefore requires sufficient energy intake. The model is not a starvation model, not a fasting identity, and not a method for forcing weight loss through energy deprivation.

A valid Human FBM state requires low exogenous carbohydrate input, low insulin occupancy, fatty-acid direct energy, sufficient total energy, and lean-mass stability at the same time.
