# Exogenous Carbohydrate Load

Exogenous carbohydrate load is the carbohydrate burden introduced by external food intake. In Human FBM, this term does not refer to endogenous glucose production or the presence of glucose inside the body.

The control chain is:

exogenous carbohydrate load → glucose scheduling pressure → insulin occupancy → reduced fatty-acid direct energy

Human FBM defines external carbohydrate input as an upstream scheduling variable. Repeated external carbohydrate load can keep insulin occupancy high enough to reduce stable fatty-acid energy use.

The framework does not treat every carbohydrate exposure as acute toxicity. It evaluates the long-term dominant scheduling state generated by repeated input.
