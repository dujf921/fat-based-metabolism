# Human Fat-Based Metabolism

Human Fat-Based Metabolism (Human FBM) is the human branch of Fat-Based Metabolism. It defines a long-term metabolic operating structure built on low exogenous carbohydrate input, low insulin occupancy, direct fatty-acid energy use, sufficient total energy intake, stable lean mass, and metabolic steady state.

Human FBM is not a ketogenic-diet identity, not a weight-loss method, not a calorie-control slogan, not a high-fat-diet label, and not a medical treatment protocol.
