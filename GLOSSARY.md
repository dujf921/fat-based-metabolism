## Fat-Based Metabolism (FBM) / 脂供能系统

A food-structure and metabolic-state framework that evaluates how dietary structure changes long-term energy regulation, substrate dominance, and downstream physiological handling. It is not a diet label, product name, or ketone-targeting protocol.

## Human Fat-Based Metabolism / 人类脂供能系统

The human branch of FBM. It defines a long-term metabolic operating structure built on low exogenous carbohydrate input, low insulin occupancy, fatty-acid direct energy, sufficient total energy, stable lean mass, and metabolic steady state.

## Feline Fat-Based Metabolism / 猫脂供能系统

The species-specific branch of FBM for obligate carnivores (cats). It evaluates feline food as structure rather than market category or isolated nutrient percentages.

## food structure / 食物结构

The integrated arrangement of macronutrient roles, processing demands, and metabolic load created by how food is composed—not an ingredient list or label claim by itself.

## metabolic fit / 代谢适配

The degree to which a given food structure aligns with a species' expected substrate dominance, insulin regulation pattern, and digestive handling capacity over time.

## primary energy substrate / 主供能底物

The macronutrient class that chronically supplies the majority of usable energy under a given food structure—evaluated by systemic operation, not by single-meal composition alone.

## exogenous carbohydrate load / 外源性碳水负荷

Carbohydrate introduced through diet that enters insulin-regulated handling pathways; its structural level affects substrate dominance and insulin occupancy. In Human FBM, this term excludes endogenous glucose production.

## insulin occupancy / 胰岛素占位

The degree to which insulin dominates long-term energy allocation. High insulin occupancy limits fatty-acid direct energy; low insulin occupancy allows stored fat and dietary fat to participate more directly in energy supply.

## insulin regulation state / 胰岛素调度状态

The chronic pattern of insulin involvement in nutrient partitioning—not a single post-meal spike, but the long-term regulatory posture shaped by food structure.

## fatty-acid direct energy / 脂肪酸直接供能

A Human FBM state in which fatty acids carry the dominant long-term energy role without repeated external carbohydrate scheduling.

## fatty acid oxidation pathway / 脂肪酸氧化路径

The mitochondrial and systemic route by which fatty acids are converted to usable energy; substrate dominance and insulin state influence how fully this pathway operates.

## body fat F / 体脂 F

The stored energy pool in the Human FBM base model. It becomes structurally accessible when insulin occupancy is low and total energy conditions allow stable mobilization.

## lean mass L / 瘦体重 L

The structural and metabolic machinery that must be preserved in the Human FBM base model. Lean-mass loss means the model has crossed an energy sufficiency or clinical boundary.

## energy intake I / 能量摄入 I

The total energy entering the system. Human FBM requires sufficient energy intake and does not treat underfeeding as a valid stable state.

## energy expenditure E / 能量消耗 E

The energy leaving the system through basal metabolism, activity, thermal cost, and other outputs. In Human FBM it is interpreted together with substrate access, not as isolated calorie arithmetic.

## energy sufficiency boundary / 能量充足边界

The boundary condition that separates Human FBM from starvation, chronic underfeeding, stress compensation, and lean-mass loss.

## protein energy pressure / 蛋白供能压力

The degree to which protein is diverted into gluconeogenic or energy-supply roles because fat or carbohydrate substrates are structurally insufficient or misaligned.

## solute load per unit energy / 单位能量溶质负荷

The renal and systemic processing burden of nitrogenous and mineral solutes relative to energy delivered—higher pressure when protein supplies excess energy or structure is misaligned.

## fat processing rate / 脂肪处理速率

The effective speed at which ingested fat moves through emulsification, enzymatic cleavage, absorption, and transport—limited by digestive structure, not by fat percentage alone.

## bile salt dispersion / 胆盐分散

The emulsification step in which bile salts break dietary fat into absorbable droplets; dispersion quality affects downstream absorption and colonic signaling.

## bile-acid-related colonic regulation / 胆汁酸相关结肠调节

Colonic motility and water-handling responses linked to bile acid flux and microbial transformation in the distal gut—not independent of upstream fat processing.

## SGLT1-mediated water-sodium absorption / SGLT1 与水钠协同吸收

Proximal small-intestinal recovery of water coupled to sodium-glucose transport; relevant to early water reclamation before colonic handling.

## sebaceous synthesis and release / 皮脂腺自身合成与释放

Local production and export of sebum by sebaceous glands—evaluated as a processing-rate and substrate-alignment output, not as passive fat leakage through skin.

## sebum processing rate / 皮脂处理速率

The balance between sebaceous output and epidermal clearance under current metabolic and local skin conditions.

## secondary microbial layer / 继发微生物层

Microbial colonization that follows altered local skin environment (e.g., excess sebum retention)—a downstream layer, not the primary structural cause.

## observable output backtrace / 可观察表现回溯

The method of tracing visible signs (stool, coat, vomiting, urine concentration) backward through mechanism layers to food structure—not treating the sign as root cause.

## clinical boundary layer / 临床边界层

The explicit limit beyond which FBM provides structural reasoning only and does not replace diagnosis, treatment, emergency care, or clinical decision-making.
