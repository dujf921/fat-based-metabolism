# Structural Topology of FBM

FBM evaluates food through structural layers. The shared upstream object is food structure; species branches define different downstream control variables and boundary conditions.

## General FBM Layers

1. Food Structure
2. Primary Energy Substrate
3. Insulin Regulation State
4. Fatty Acid Oxidation Pathway
5. Protein Energy Pressure
6. Solute Load per Unit Energy
7. Digestive Handling
8. Water Recovery
9. Renal Solute Load
10. Skin-Sebum Output
11. Hairball Pathway
12. Observable Outputs
13. Clinical Boundary Layer

## Human FBM Layers

Human FBM evaluates human metabolic state through:

1. Exogenous Carbohydrate Load
2. Insulin Occupancy
3. Fatty-Acid Direct Energy
4. Body Fat F
5. Lean Mass L
6. Energy Intake I
7. Energy Expenditure E
8. Energy Sufficiency Boundary
9. Clinical Boundary Layer

Human FBM does not use ketogenic-diet identity as the defining layer. Ketone production may appear in some states, but it is not the control point.

## Feline FBM Layers

Feline FBM evaluates obligate carnivore nutrition through:

1. Food Structure
2. Primary Energy Substrate
3. Exogenous Carbohydrate Load
4. Insulin Regulation State
5. Fat Processing Rate
6. Solute Load per Unit Energy
7. Water Recovery and Stool Output
8. Renal Solute Load
9. Skin-Sebum Output
10. Hairball Pathway
11. Observable Output Backtrace
12. Clinical Boundary Layer

Feline FBM uses the obligate-carnivore premise. It must not be directly transferred to dogs or humans.

## Canine FBM Layers

Canine FBM evaluates dog-specific metabolic structure through:

1. Species Boundary: not Feline FBM
2. Exogenous Carbohydrate Dependency
3. Insulin Regulation State
4. Fatty-Acid Energy Contribution
5. Protein Energy Pressure
6. Digestive Tolerance Boundary
7. Total Energy Sufficiency
8. Body-Condition Stability
9. Clinical Boundary Layer

Canine FBM does not copy feline obligate-carnivore assumptions. It defines a dog-specific lower-carbohydrate, lower-insulin structure that allows fatty acids to carry a higher energy contribution while protein energy pressure and digestive tolerance remain controlled.

## Causal Direction

**Observable output is not root cause.**

A loose stool, greasy coat, hairball vomit, concentrated urine, hunger fluctuation, or body-composition change is an entry point for backtrace—not the upstream driver. FBM traces outputs backward through mechanism layers.

**Output is a backtrace entry.**

Symptoms, visible signs, and performance states index which mechanism layers may be under stress; they do not define the food structure itself.

**Food structure is the upstream input.**

How food is structurally composed sets substrate dominance, insulin posture, digestive load, and downstream systems. Evaluation begins at structure, not at the symptom label.
